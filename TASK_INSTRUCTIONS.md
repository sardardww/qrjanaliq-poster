## 1. Strict High-Signal Curation Rules ("Eng Qiziqarli va Muhimlari")

Never take every post from a source channel indiscriminately:
1. **Appointments (`#Tayınlaw`):** ONLY publish major, high-impact, or uniquely notable appointments — e.g., Ministers, Joqarǵı Keńes / Council of Ministers leadership, Hokims, Republic-level agency heads, or historic firsts. **Skip** routine minor deputies, district department staff, or dry protocol shuffling.
2. **Official Meetings:** Skip dry bureaucratic "a meeting was held" reports unless a **concrete decision affecting citizens** was announced (new train/flight, salary/pension raise, gas/electricity limit, subsidy, student quota, visa-free regime, road opening).
3. **Zero Fluff / Zero Ads:** Never post paid ads, "Qayırli tań" greetings, cooking videos, unverified rumors, or clickbait.
4. **Freshness & Deduplication:** Focus on news from the **last 1–3 hours** (or timeless high-utility legal/citizen guides when breaking news is quiet). Always inspect recent `@qrjanaliq` posts before publishing so no topic is ever repeated.
